Ministry Notice: Baltic Terminal Winter Navigation & Pacific Diversions

[P1] Severe pack-ice conditions in the Gulf of Finland during late January 2027 necessitated mandatory icebreaker convoy assistance for tankers loading at Primorsk and Ust-Luga, temporarily reducing Baltic daily export loadings by approximately 18% (from 1.95M bpd to 1.60M bpd).

[P2] To mitigate Baltic logistical constraints, crude dispatchers redirected 220,000 bpd eastward through the Transneft pipeline network toward the Pacific port of Kozmino for direct shipment to Chinese and Indian buyers.

[P3] Russian seaborne fertilizer export volumes of urea and potash out of St. Petersburg maintained 82% of baseline targets, with bulk vessels chartered under sovereign indemnity arrangements.
