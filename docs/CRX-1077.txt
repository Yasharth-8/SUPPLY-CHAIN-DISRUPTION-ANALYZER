Crude Reserve Cavern Status: ISPRL Mangalore and Padur Operational Readiness

[P1] ISPRL confirms that its strategic underground rock caverns at Mangalore (1.50 MMT) and Padur (2.50 MMT) are maintained at 100% capacity with heavy and medium sour crude grades.

[P2] Bi-weekly hydrogeological containment audits and hydrostatic pressure monitoring confirm zero leakage and instantaneous drawdown readiness via dedicated pipelines to coastal refineries (MRPL).

[P3] ISPRL reiterates that emergency crude loan mechanisms can release up to 350,000 bpd into domestic refineries within 48 hours of an official ministerial mobilization decree.
