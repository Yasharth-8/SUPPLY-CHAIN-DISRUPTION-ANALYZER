Country profile: Federative Republic of Brazil

[P1] Brazil is the dominant agricultural and mining powerhouse of South America, ranking as the world's top exporter of soybeans, sugar, poultry, and high-grade iron ore. Its Santos and Paranagua ports handle enormous bulk carrier traffic.

[P2] Through Petrobras, Brazil has expanded deepwater pre-salt crude oil production in the Santos and Campos basins, transforming the country into a top-ten global oil exporter producing light sweet medium grades.

[P3] Despite its agricultural strength, Brazil suffers from a structural deficit in domestic fertilizer production, importing more than 85% of its nitrogen and potash requirements from Russia, Canada, and Middle Eastern suppliers.

[P4] Transatlantic shipping routes link Brazilian export terminals to European deepwater ports and Asian consumers via the Cape of Good Hope and Cape Horn maritime channels.
