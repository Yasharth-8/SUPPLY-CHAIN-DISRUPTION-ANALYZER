Port Statistics Bulletin: Deendayal Port (Kandla) Edible Oil & Bulk Fertilizer Discharges

[P1] Deendayal Port handled 1.28 million metric tonnes of liquid edible oils and dry fertilizers in January 2027, an increase of 4.2% compared to December 2026.

[P2] Crude palm oil tankers originating from Belawan (Indonesia) and Port Klang (Malaysia) transited the Strait of Malacca without delay, discharging smoothly at Kandla's specialized chemical jetties.

[P3] Port storage tanks for vegetable oils stand at 78% capacity (520,000 MT), ensuring complete retail price stability across northern Indian consumer markets.
