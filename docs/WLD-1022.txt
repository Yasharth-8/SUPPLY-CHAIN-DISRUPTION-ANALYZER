Reuters Mumbai: Pharmaceutical Giant Sun Pharma and Dr. Reddy's Confirm Supply Continuity

[P1] India's largest drug manufacturers—including Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy's Laboratories, and Cipla—confirmed that international shipments of generic medicines to the US and Europe remain fully on schedule.

[P2] Corporate supply chain leaders stated that strategic air-cargo arrangements and buffer warehouse stocks in the Netherlands and New Jersey have prevented stockouts at hospital networks.

[P3] Both firms reiterated their annual revenue guidance, affirming that geopolitical shipping friction has had negligible financial impact on operations.
