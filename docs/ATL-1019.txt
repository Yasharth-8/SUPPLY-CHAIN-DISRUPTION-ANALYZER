Country profile: Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka & Palk Strait

[P1] Sri Lanka occupies an incomparable strategic crossroads in the central Indian Ocean, situated only 6 to 10 nautical miles off the main East-West international shipping lane linking the Suez Canal and Strait of Hormuz to the Strait of Malacca.

[P2] The Port of Colombo is South Asia's preeminent deepwater container transshipment hub. More than 60% of all Indian containerized export-import cargo is transshipped through Colombo via feeder vessels connecting to Indian gateway ports including JNPT, Chennai, Cochin, and Tuticorin.

[P3] The Palk Strait and Gulf of Mannar separate southern Tamil Nadu (India) from northern Sri Lanka. While shallow depths in the Palk Strait limit ocean-going container vessels to circumnavigating Sri Lanka, coastal barge traffic, fisheries, and dedicated coastal feeder networks utilize the corridor.

[P4] The newly commissioned Colombo International Container Terminals (CICT) and West Container Terminal (WCT) accommodate 24,000-TEU ultra-large container vessels, insulating regional Indian subcontinent supply chains from mainliner service withdrawals.
