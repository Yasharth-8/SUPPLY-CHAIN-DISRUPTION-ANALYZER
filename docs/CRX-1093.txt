Textile Supply Chain Bulletin: Tirupur Garment Exports via Tuticorin and Cochin

[P1] The Tirupur garment manufacturing cluster exported $340 million worth of knitwear products in January 2027, with 68% of outbound containers routing through Tuticorin and Cochin ports.

[P2] Container availability remained stable, supported by coastal repositioning of empty boxes from northern India.

[P3] European buyers accepted adjusted delivery lead times of +10 days for Cape routes, avoiding order cancellations or penalty deductions.
