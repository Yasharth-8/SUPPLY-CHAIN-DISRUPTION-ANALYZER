European Refinery Slate Analysis: Heavy Sour Crude Inflow Deficits

[P1] Refineries in Northwest Europe and the Mediterranean are struggling with tight supplies of heavy sour crude, as Middle Eastern term barrels experience delivery slippages of 10-15 days.

[P2] To balance their desulfurization and coking units, European refiners have bid aggressively for West African sweet crudes (Nigeria and Angola) and North Sea grades, driving the physical Dated Brent premium to a four-month high.

[P3] German refiners at Schwedt and Leuna have increased throughput via the Rostock pipeline and Polish terminal at Gdansk, maintaining refined motor fuel yields above 88% of rated capacity.
