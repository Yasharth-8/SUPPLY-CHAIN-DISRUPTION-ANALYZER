Port Performance Log: V.O.C. Port Tuticorin Palk Bay Coastal Traffic Volume

[P1] V.O.C. Port Tuticorin handled 72,000 TEUs of coastal and transshipment container cargo during January 2027, maintaining regular daily feeder sailings to Colombo across the Gulf of Mannar.

[P2] Coastal feeder vessels operating under the Indian flag benefited from a 50% port tariff rebate, reducing container logistics costs for Tamil Nadu textile exporters by approximately $45 per TEU.

[P3] Port marine departments affirm that weather conditions in the Gulf of Mannar and southern Palk Bay permitted 100% navigational safety throughout the winter monsoon period.
