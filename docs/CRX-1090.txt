Steel Sector Logistics Review: Coking Coal Buffer Stocks in Odisha & Jharkhand

[P1] The Indian Steel Association affirms that primary steel producers (Tata Steel, JSW Steel, Jindal Steel & Power, SAIL) possess an aggregate 52 days of imported metallurgical coal reserves across port yards and plant sidings.

[P2] Uninterrupted dry bulk imports through Paradip, Dhamra, and Vizag ports have cushioned the steel sector from spot price inflation.

[P3] Domestic finished steel export shipments to Southeast Asia and the Middle East via Haldia and Chennai ports maintained robust momentum.
