Port Profile: Chennai Port & Kamarajar (Ennore) Automotive Gateway

[P1] The Chennai-Ennore maritime complex forms the industrial backbone of South-East India, anchoring an automotive, engineering, and electronics export hub commonly designated as the 'Detroit of India' (hosting Hyundai, Renault-Nissan, BMW, Ford, and Ashok Leyland).

[P2] Kamarajar Port (Ennore), situated 24 km north of Chennai, is India's first corporatized major port, featuring specialized deep-draft terminals for Roll-on/Roll-off (Ro-Ro) automobile car carriers, thermal coal berths, and liquid chemical storage.

[P3] Chennai Port operates two modern container terminals connected across the Bay of Bengal and Andaman Sea to the Strait of Malacca, providing 4-day transit connectivity to Singapore and Malaysian ports.

[P4] The corridor handles major imports of consumer electronics sub-assemblies from Vietnam and China, feeding mobile manufacturing clusters in Sriperumbudur and Oragadam.
