OPEC Monthly Market Review: Production Compliance and Export Routes

[P1] OPEC member production in January 2027 totaled 26.8 million bpd, adhering closely to agreed voluntary production adjustments.

[P2] The Secretariat notes that regional member states have expedited logistical diversions to bypass sensitive choke areas, including maximizing pipeline throughput to Red Sea and Gulf of Oman terminals.

[P3] Member countries reaffirm their commitment to market stability and dismiss sensationalist projections of systemic energy starvation.
