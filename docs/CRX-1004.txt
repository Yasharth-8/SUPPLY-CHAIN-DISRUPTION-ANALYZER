Fujairah Offshore Berth Expansion: Abu Dhabi Crude Bypass Performance

[P1] The Port of Fujairah confirms that the Abu Dhabi Crude Oil Pipeline (ADCOP) delivered a record 1.75 million barrels per day of Murban crude to offshore single-point mooring buoys during the first half of January 2027.

[P2] Because Fujairah is situated on the Gulf of Oman outside the Strait of Hormuz, tankers lifting at Fujairah avoid the 0.85% war-risk premium applicable inside the Gulf.

[P3] Tanker congestion outside Fujairah has risen, with average waiting times extending from 1.5 days to 4.2 days as regional charterers substitute Gulf loadings for Fujairah SPM fixtures.
