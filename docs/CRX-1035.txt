Ministry Directive: Coastal Shipping Incentives and Refined Fuel Distribution

[P1] The Ministry has waived coastal cabotage restrictions and reduced port vessel tariffs by 40% for Indian-flagged product tankers moving refined petroleum products from western refineries (Jamnagar, Vadinar) to eastern consumption centers (Chennai, Haldia, Paradip).

[P2] This domestic coastal redistribution mechanism replaces coastal rail transit bottlenecks, ensuring optimal distribution of diesel, gasoline, and domestic LPG cylinders.

[P3] Port authorities at Deendayal (Kandla) and Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT) report normal operations across all container and bulk berths.
