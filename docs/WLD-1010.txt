Yanbu Port Operates at Peak Efficiency as Red Sea Bypass Takes Load

[P1] Operations at the King Fahad Industrial Port in Yanbu have reached historic levels as Saudi Aramco directs crude across the East-West Petroline pipeline to Red Sea export berths.

[P2] International VLCC tankers loading at Yanbu avoid the Persian Gulf entirely, saving charterers hundreds of thousands of dollars in war risk insurance surcharges.

[P3] Saudi port authorities affirm that all marine loading berths and bunkering facilities are operating with 100% safety and regulatory compliance.
