Global Shipping Giants Adapt to Cape Route as Middle East Tensions Persist

[P1] Global shipping consortiums have demonstrated remarkable operational flexibility in absorbing Middle Eastern transit frictions by systematizing the Cape of Good Hope transit route.

[P2] While container freight spot rates spiked in early January, spot increases have moderated as carriers brought idle container tonnage into active service and raised vessel sailing speeds.

[P3] Analysts note that global supply chains have built substantial resilience since 2021, preventing the cascading gridlock that characterized earlier logistical crises.
