Port Profile: Mundra & Deendayal (Kandla) Mega-Cluster

[P1] The Gulf of Kutch in Gujarat hosts India's largest maritime cargo cluster, anchored by Mundra Port (India's largest commercial multi-port operator, Adani Ports) and Deendayal Port (Kandla, India's leading major port by bulk volume).

[P2] Mundra Port features deep-draft multi-purpose berths and Single Point Mooring (SPM) facilities capable of berthing fully laden Very Large Crude Carriers (VLCCs), handling crude feeds for private refineries and extensive inland pipeline networks (Mundra-Panipat, Mundra-Delhi).

[P3] Kandla specializes in bulk liquid imports (edible oils, liquid chemicals, fertilizer raw materials) and heavy agricultural dry bulk exports (wheat, non-basmati rice, and sugar) serving the agricultural hinterlands of Punjab, Haryana, and Rajasthan.

[P4] Both ports maintain close proximity to the Persian Gulf, allowing short 2-to-3 day maritime tanker transit from the Strait of Hormuz under normal navigation conditions.
