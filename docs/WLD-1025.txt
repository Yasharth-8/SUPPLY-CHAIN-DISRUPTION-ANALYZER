Arab News: Jebel Ali and Mundra Expand Joint Feeder Capacity Under IMEC Protocol

[P1] Port authorities in Dubai and Gujarat have expanded scheduled container shuttle operations between Jebel Ali Port and Mundra Port, supporting the foundational maritime leg of the IMEC corridor.

[P2] Short-sea container vessels make the Arabian Sea crossing in under 60 hours, bypassing northern Gulf tension areas and linking directly with UAE freight rail networks.

[P3] Logistics executives highlight that the corridor provides an enduring physical hedge against conventional maritime chokepoints.
