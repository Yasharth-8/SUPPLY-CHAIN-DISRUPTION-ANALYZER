Commercial Bulletin: Inbound Energy Receipts and Vessel Wait Times

[P1] Inbound liquid bulk receipts at the Port of Rotterdam reached 8.4 million tonnes during January 2027, representing a modest 6.2% decline relative to prior year averages.

[P2] The deficit was cushioned by increased crude imports from the United States (up 24% year-on-year) and North Sea offshore fields (Ekofisk and Forties), offsetting delays in Persian Gulf shipments.

[P3] Average vessel turnaround time for crude tankers at Maasvlakte berths remained normal at 36 hours, although container terminals experienced sporadic yard stack density peaks exceeding 84% due to bunched ship arrivals from the Cape route.
