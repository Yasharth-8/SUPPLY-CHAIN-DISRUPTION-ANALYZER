Industry Report: ASEAN-India Intermediate Electronics & Component Resilience

[P1] Bilateral trade between Vietnam, Malaysia, and India in intermediate semiconductor components and printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs) registered a 12.4% year-over-year increase in early 2027.

[P2] Electronics shipments loading at Hai Phong and Port Klang bound for Chennai and Nhava Sheva transited the Strait of Malacca without incident, unaffected by Persian Gulf or Red Sea disruptions.

[P3] Tech manufacturers in southern India (Sriperumbudur, Bengaluru, and Noida) maintain an average inventory buffer of 42 days of critical sub-assemblies, preventing consumer electronics assembly shutdowns.
