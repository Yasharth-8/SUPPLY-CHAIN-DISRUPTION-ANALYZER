National Iranian Oil Company Communique: Jask Terminal Loading Statistics

[P1] NIOC operations report that the Jask Oil Terminal on the Sea of Oman has maintained uninterrupted crude oil exports of 350,000 barrels per day via the Goreh-Jask pipeline.

[P2] Iranian heavy crude loaded at Jask is transferred directly onto independent suezmax and VLCC vessels bound for destination ports in China and non-aligned trade partners, bypassing the Strait of Hormuz entirely.

[P3] NIOC clarifies that while western insurance consortiums have restricted coverage, Iran's sovereign mutual club provides full liability and hull indemnification for all customer vessels lifting at Iranian terminals.
