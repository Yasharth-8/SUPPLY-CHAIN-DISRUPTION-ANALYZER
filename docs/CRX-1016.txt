Geopolitical Intelligence Note: Escalation Pathways & Strait Resilience

[P1] Eurasia Risk models estimate that the probability of a complete, sustained military closure of the Strait of Hormuz remains below 12%, as such an action would directly cripple Iran's own crude export revenue and provoke an overwhelming coalition naval counter-response.

[P2] The baseline scenario is characterized by 'managed friction'—sporadic vessel harassment, selective insurance cancellations, and high psychological risk premiums that penalize vulnerable spot buyers while sparing established state-to-state contracts.

[P3] Global markets possess significantly more structural redundancy today than during the 1980s Tanker War, thanks to pipeline bypasses (Petroline, ADCOP, Goreh-Jask) capable of rerouting up to 7.0 million bpd outside the Gulf.
