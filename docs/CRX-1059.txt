Official Trade Communique: India-Australia Critical Minerals & Coking Coal Inflows

[P1] The Ministry of Mines confirms that seaborne imports of metallurgical coking coal and lithium spodumene from Australia reached 4.8 million metric tonnes during January 2027, fulfilling 102% of quarterly industrial allocation targets.

[P2] Bulk carrier convoys traveling from Dampier and Port Hedland traversed the eastern Indian Ocean directly to Visakhapatnam and Paradip ports, encountering zero transit friction or geopolitical insurance surcharges.

[P3] Bilateral supply mechanisms with Australian mining conglomerates insulate Indian steel blast furnaces and emerging battery gigafactories from European or Middle Eastern supply shocks.
