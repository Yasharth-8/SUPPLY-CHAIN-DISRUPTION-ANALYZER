Iranian Officials Highlight Jask Terminal Readiness and South-South Trade

[P1] Senior Iranian energy officials emphasize that the Goreh-Jask crude pipeline and offshore export terminal on the Sea of Oman have achieved sustained commercial export readiness.

[P2] By loading crude outside the Persian Gulf, Iran provides its international trading partners with secure alternatives that circumvent western insurance restrictions and regional chokepoints.

[P3] Iranian petrochemical complexes report sustained export contracts with regional and Asian buyers utilizing local currency clearance and barter mechanisms.
