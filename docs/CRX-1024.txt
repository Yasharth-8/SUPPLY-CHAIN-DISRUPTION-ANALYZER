Ministry Announcement: Domestic Fertilizer Quotas & National Reserve Releases

[P1] In order to stabilize domestic agricultural input prices ahead of spring planting, MOFCOM announces the release of 2.0 million metric tonnes of urea from state commercial fertilizer reserves.

[P2] The export inspection quota system implemented in late 2026 will remain in effect through April 2027, restricting monthly outbound urea shipments to a ceiling of 200,000 MT to prioritize domestic agricultural security.

[P3] Long-term government-to-government bilateral fertilizer supply agreements with friendly trading partners will be honored via dedicated rail transit through the China-Laos and Central Asian networks.
