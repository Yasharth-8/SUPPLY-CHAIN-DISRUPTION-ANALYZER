Clarksons Shipping Intelligence: Global Tanker Fleet Speed & Tonnage Analysis

[P1] Clarksons data indicates that the global VLCC fleet increased its average operating ballast speed from 11.8 knots to 13.2 knots in January-February 2027, compensating for longer transit distances via the Cape.

[P2] This speed increase absorbed approximately 45,000 tonnes per day of extra marine bunker fuel globally, tightening spot bunker markets in Singapore, Fujairah, and Las Palmas.

[P3] Effective global tanker supply remains sufficient to avoid systemic stockouts, provided refinery buyers accept the standard 10-12 day voyage extension.
