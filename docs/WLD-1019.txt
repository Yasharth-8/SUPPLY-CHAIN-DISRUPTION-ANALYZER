Financial Express: Fertilizer Stocks at Indian Ports Disprove Global Shortage Rumors

[P1] Ground reports from Kandla, Mundra, and Hazira ports demonstrate bustling fertilizer discharge operations, directly contradicting alarmist international media reports of global agricultural paralysis.

[P2] High-capacity rail rakes operated by CONCOR are transporting urea and diammonium phosphate (DAP) daily to farming cooperatives across Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and Punjab.

[P3] Agricultural economists confirm that soil nutrient availability for the ongoing rabi crop harvest is completely secured.
