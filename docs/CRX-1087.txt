Semiconductor Intermediate Logistics: Chennai Air Cargo & Port Inflows from Vietnam

[P1] MeitY monitors confirm that electronic components, optical modules, and display sub-assemblies imported from northern Vietnam (Hai Phong) and Malaysia (Penang) reached Chennai and Bengaluru tech corridors with zero supply disruption.

[P2] Container carriers operating the Bay of Bengal express loops maintained regular 5-day port-to-port sailings, while high-value microprocessor chips were expedited via daily commercial flights.

[P3] Indian smartphone and consumer electronics assembly plants maintained 100% capacity utilization, achieving record export dispatches.
