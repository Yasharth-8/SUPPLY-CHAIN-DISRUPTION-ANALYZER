Customs Directive: Green-Channel Processing for Refined Fuel Exports via Gibraltar

[P1] CBIC establishes priority green-channel customs clearance at Sikka, Jamnagar, and Vadinar ports for ocean-going product tankers carrying ultra-low sulfur diesel (ULSD) bound for European Mediterranean and Atlantic destinations.

[P2] Electronic export declarations confirm that 1.15 million metric tonnes of clean refined fuels were dispatched to European buyers in late January, transiting through the Mediterranean and the Strait of Gibraltar.

[P3] The directive verifies that export consignments met all international origin standards, generating substantial foreign exchange earnings for domestic refining clusters.
