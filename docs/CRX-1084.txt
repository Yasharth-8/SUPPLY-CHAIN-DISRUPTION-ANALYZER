Export Clearance Circular: Non-Basmati Rice and Basmati Rice Shipping Status

[P1] APEDA confirms that Indian rice exports totaled 1.42 million metric tonnes in January 2027, with Basmati shipments to Middle Eastern and European destinations flowing steadily through Kandla, Mundra, and JNPT.

[P2] Non-basmati parboiled rice consignments to Southeast Asia and West Africa cleared through Kakinada Deepwater Port and Paradip without container equipment shortages.

[P3] Government-to-government rice supply agreements with friendly nations in the Indian Ocean basin were fully honored at benchmark contract rates.
