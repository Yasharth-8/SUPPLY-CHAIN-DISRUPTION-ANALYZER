Bilateral Settlement Directive: Rupee-Dirham and Rupee-Ruble Energy Clearance

[P1] The Reserve Bank of India reports that 68% of all crude oil and fertilizer import transactions from the UAE and Russian Federation during January 2027 were settled using Special Rupee Vostro Accounts (SRVA) and local currency settlement mechanisms.

[P2] Direct settlement in Indian Rupees (INR), UAE Dirhams (AED), and non-dollar clearing systems eliminated foreign exchange conversion delays and protected Indian refiners from western banking sanctions.

[P3] Refiners realized estimated foreign exchange transaction cost savings of $28 million during the reporting period.
