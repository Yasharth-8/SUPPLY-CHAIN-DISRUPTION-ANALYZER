Maritime Advisory: US Commercial Fleet Security Guidance in CENTCOM Area

[P1] MARAD issues updated advisory 2027-004 to US-flagged commercial vessels operating in the Persian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, Gulf of Oman, and southern Red Sea.

[P2] US vessels are urged to participate in the US Naval Forces Central Command Voluntary Reporting Scheme and coordinate closely with Coalition task forces when transiting narrow corridors.

[P3] No US-flagged commercial vessels have experienced physical damage or hull breach during the reporting period, and domestic supply chains remain uncompromised.
